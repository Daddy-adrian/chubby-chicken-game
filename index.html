🎮 How to Play

Answer English grammar questions correctly to feed your chubby chicken! With each correct answer, the chicken inflates by +5% scale (up to 2.2x) and lets out a joyful "Bawk-bawk-bawk!" cluck. Hit a 10-answer streak to trigger the celebratory arpeggio flight takeoff, burst confetti, and promote your chicken to rare skin tiers!

✍️ Active Writing & Word Bank

Type your answer directly into the sentence blank and press Enter (or tap Check / בדוק). You can also tap any reference pill in the Word Bank below to auto-fill the blank instantly.

🎯 Two Rich Learning Levels

Switch anytime between 🟢 Level 1: A1 Beginner (everyday vocabulary, home, family, school, and daily routines) and 🟣 Level 2: Band III Grade 12 (official Ministry Bagrut exam vocabulary & idioms).

🔍 Interactive Hebrew Dictionary & Audio

Every word in the displayed sentence is an interactive token. Tap any word to listen to native English speech pronunciation and inspect its grammatical part of speech and Hebrew translation.

✨ 7 Core Grammar Modules Included in Both Levels:

  • Past Simple: Regular/irregular verbs, did / did not negation and question inversion.
  • Present Simple: Third-person -s/-es, do / does auxiliaries for habits and daily life.
  • Future Simple: Will / will not predictive assertions and inquiries.
  • Present Progressive: Auxiliary be (am/is/are) + -ing participle for concurrent actions.
  • Articles (A vs An): Phonetic vowel sound contrast (e.g. an orange, an egg, an incentive vs a dog, a firm, a unique).
  • State & Location (Copula Be): Non-action descriptors, spatial relations, and adjective complements.
  • Prepositions & Idioms: High-frequency prepositions (at, behind, by, for) and collocations (step by step, in advance, at the expense of).
